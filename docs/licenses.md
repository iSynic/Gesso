## Notes

- Entries such as `LGPL-2.1-or-later` (r-efi) appear because that crate is triple-licensed
  (`MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR LGPL-2.1-or-later`); we take the permissive option.
- `memchr` is `Unlicense OR MIT`; we take MIT.
- `bench`, `xtask` and `memory-stats` are tools and don't ship in the app.
- No MPL, GPL-only or other copyleft crate is in the product tree.
- Stage 4 added the renderer stack (vello, vello_cpu, wgpu, naga, image and their dependencies).
  New license kinds: BSD-2-Clause (zerocopy), BSD-3-Clause (moxcms, pxfm, via `image`), 0BSD
  (adler2, dual with MIT/Apache), all permissive.
- Stage 5 added the canvas app's stack: winit, egui / egui-winit / egui-wgpu, AccessKit, rfd,
  arboard (via egui-winit) and their platform crates. New license kinds, all permissive or taken
  under a permissive option:
  - BSL-1.0 (clipboard-win, error-code), permissive;
  - Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception (rustix, linux-raw-sys, wasip2, wit-bindgen);
  - `self_cell` is `Apache-2.0 OR GPL-2.0-only`, so we take Apache-2.0.
- `dirs` was tried in Stage 5 and removed: its dependency `option-ext` is MPL-2.0 only. The
  editor resolves its per-user folders itself (`crates/editor/src/dirs.rs`). `deny.toml` still
  allows MPL-2.0 for unmodified dependencies, but none is in the tree.
- The canvas app reads system fonts at run time (Segoe UI, Segoe UI Symbol, Yu Gothic on Windows)
  as UI fallbacks; they are not bundled or redistributed.
- `vello_encoding@0.11.0` comes from `third_party/vello_encoding` (a patched copy of the crates.io
  release, same MIT OR Apache-2.0 license, license files kept); see `third_party/README.md`.
- Stage 6 added the agent API's stack: rquickjs (QuickJS, MIT), interprocess (0BSD OR
  Apache-2.0, with recvmsg and doctest-file, 0BSD), rmcp (MIT, without its macros crate), tokio
  and schemars. No new license kinds.
- Stage 7b added export and SVG import: pdf-writer and subsetter (MIT OR Apache-2.0), usvg
  and its parsers (roxmltree, svgtypes, simplecss, data-url, fontdb,
  unicode-*: MIT OR Apache-2.0, Apache-2.0 or MIT), `image`'s JPEG and WebP codecs. New license
  kinds: none (`tiny-skia-path` is BSD-3-Clause, `ryu` is taken under BSL-1.0 or Apache-2.0).
  `resvg`, the independent SVG renderer the tests compare against, is a dev-dependency only.
- `vello@0.11.0` likewise comes from `third_party/vello` (a patched copy, same Apache-2.0 OR MIT
  license, license files kept), since 2026-10-29; see `third_party/README.md`.
